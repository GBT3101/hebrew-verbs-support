About the app

Hebrew Verbs: The Course teaches Hebrew roots and the seven Binyanim with the videos, readings and quizzes of the Hebrew Grammar Verbs Master Course, and adds Infinite Practice with 98 recorded verb examples. It runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 16.4 or later, and on Android phones and tablets with Android 8.0 or later.

The Intro to Hebrew Verbs and the Paal section are free. One purchase unlocks the rest of the course, including the Ultimate Summary Section and Infinite Practice. It is a one-time purchase that unlocks all paid sections permanently on every device signed in to your Apple Account. No subscription.

Common questions

Does the app work offline?

Yes. All videos, readings, quizzes and recordings are inside the app, so the whole course works without an internet connection. Nothing needs to download on first launch.

How do I keep my progress on another iPhone or iPad?

Sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud on both devices. The app saves your progress on the device and syncs it through your own iCloud account. Syncing can take a few minutes, and it waits until the device is online. There is no separate app account.

I bought the full course. How do I unlock it on another device?

Use the same Apple Account for App Store purchases. The course usually unlocks by itself. If it does not, open Settings & About (the gear on the Home screen) and tap Restore Purchases.

I use the Android app. How do I keep my progress and my purchase?

Progress is saved on your device. If device backup is turned on in your Android settings, Android backs it up to your Google account and restores it when you reinstall the app or set up a new phone. The purchase belongs to your Google Play account: install the app with the same account and it unlocks by itself, or open Settings & About (the gear on the Home screen) and tap Restore Purchases. A purchase made in the Apple App Store does not transfer to Android, and the reverse.

Can my family share the purchase?

No. The full-course purchase does not support Family Sharing.

How do I ask for a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. If a refund is granted, the paid sections lock again on your devices once the App Store reports it. Your progress is kept.

On Android, purchases are handled by Google Play. Request a refund through your Google Play order history (Google Play refund help). If a refund is granted, the paid sections lock again once Google Play reports it. Your progress is kept.

What happens if I switch to a different iCloud account?

Each iCloud account keeps its own progress. Switching back to the earlier account brings its progress back.
