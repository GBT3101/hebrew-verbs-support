Effective October 11, 2026. Android section added October 13, 2026.

In short

  • The developer does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party code in the app.
  • All course content is inside the app and works offline.
  • Your learning progress is saved on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account. The developer cannot see it.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. The developer never sees your payment details.

Course content

The videos, readings, quizzes and recordings are bundled with the app. The app does not download content and does not contact any server run by the developer. The developer does not run any server for the app.

Your learning progress

To let you continue where you left off, the app saves:

  • which lessons you opened, finished or skipped, and when;
  • the second where you stopped each video;
  • your quiz answers and scores;
  • the question you are on in Infinite Practice and your answer to it;
  • a random identifier created when the app is installed. It contains nothing about you or your device and is used only to merge progress from your different devices.

This is stored in the app's private storage on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, the same progress is also kept in your own iCloud account using Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so it syncs to your other devices with the same Apple Account. That copy belongs to your iCloud account; the developer has no access to it.

How the app tells iCloud accounts apart

So that each iCloud account keeps its own progress, the app asks Apple's CloudKit for your iCloud account status and for an identifier that Apple creates for this app only. The app keeps only a one-way hash of that identifier on your device. It stores no records in CloudKit.

Purchases

The full-course purchase uses Apple's App Store (StoreKit). Apple processes the payment. The app receives the transaction from Apple to confirm the unlock and keeps a small record on your device (whether the course is unlocked, the transaction number and the date it was confirmed), so the course stays unlocked when you are offline. This record is not sent anywhere.

Apple services

iCloud and the App Store are provided by Apple under Apple's own terms and privacy policy. If you choose in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide the developer with crash reports and usage statistics that Apple has made anonymous. The app itself does not include any analytics.

Your choices

  • If you do not use iCloud, progress stays on your device only. The app works fully without iCloud.
  • Deleting the app removes the progress and the purchase record stored on that device. Your purchase stays with your Apple Account and can be restored.
  • The copy of your progress in your iCloud account is not removed when you delete the app. If you want help with it, email us.

Android app

This section applies to the Android version of the app, available on Google Play. The iOS text above and below applies to the iPhone and iPad version.

  • No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. The Android app has no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking. The developer does not run a server for the app and does not collect personal data.
  • Progress on your device. The app saves the same progress described above (lessons opened, finished or skipped, video position, quiz answers and scores, your Infinite Practice question and the random install identifier) in its private storage on your device.
  • Android backup. If you have device backup turned on in your Android settings, Android's Auto Backup may copy this progress to your own Google account so it can be restored when you reinstall the app or set up a new phone. Android, not the app, makes and restores that copy; it belongs to your Google account and the developer cannot see it. Nothing else from the app is included in the backup.
  • Purchases through Google Play. The full-course purchase uses Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment under Google's own terms and privacy policy, and the developer never sees your payment details. The app receives only the purchase state and purchase token from Google Play to confirm the unlock, and keeps a small record on your device (whether the course is unlocked and when it was confirmed) so the course stays unlocked offline. This record is not sent anywhere. A purchase made in the Apple App Store does not carry over to Android, and the reverse.
  • Deleting your data. Uninstalling the app removes the progress and purchase record on that device. To remove the backup copy, delete the app's backup in your Google account (in Android settings, Google, Backup, or in your Google Drive backups). Your purchase stays with your Google account and can be restored by installing the app again.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.
